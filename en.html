Catown Map

Watch over community cats. Connect them with care.

Catown Map helps people safely share sightings of cats living in their community. By noticing changes and signs that a cat may need help, we can create opportunities for ongoing care, rescue, and support.

App features and Google Sign-In

Catown Map lets people view and share community cat sightings on a map and use favorites, comments, and voting to support ongoing local care.

If you choose Google Sign-In, Catown Map requests only your name, email address, and profile picture to create and authenticate your account. We do not sell this information or use it for advertising.
